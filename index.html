🏏 6-Over Turf Cricket League

Rules & Regulations

💰 1. Tournament Basics

  • 💵Entry Fee: ₹1,500 per team.
  • 👥Team Squad: 8 members per team.
  • 🏃Fielding: All 8 players are allowed to play and field.

⏱️ 2. Match Format & Time

  • 🏏Innings: 6 overs per side.
  • 🎯Bowling Quota (2+1+1+1+1): One bowler may bowl a maximum of 2 overs; four other bowlers must bowl 1 over each.
  • ⏳Strict Time Limit: Teams must complete their overs within the allotted time.
  • ⚠️Overs Reduction: If time is limited or a match exceeds the allotted time, organizers may reduce the overs to keep the tournament on schedule.
  • 🤝Teams are expected to cooperate with umpires and organizers to avoid delays.

📋 3. Standard Turf Rules

  • 👨‍⚖️Umpire's Decision: Final and binding. No arguments or disputes.
  • ➕Wides & No-Balls: Each counts as 1 extra run and an extra delivery.
  • 🎯Free Hit: A front-foot no-ball gives a free hit. Any full toss above waist height is a No-Ball and the next delivery is a Free Hit.
  • 🚫LBW: LBW dismissals are not applicable.
  • 🏃Byes & Overthrows: Runs from byes and overthrows are allowed. Leg byes are not allowed.
  • 🤝Throwing & Chucking: Allowed, to avoid unnecessary conflicts during the match.
  • 🥅Scoring Zones: Runs are awarded per the turf scoring zones and net markers. Hitting the straight net with a bounce may count as 4 or 6, depending on the turf markers.

📢 4. Reporting Time

  • 🕒All teams must report to the venue 15 minutes before their scheduled match time.
  • ⚠️Late arrivals may result in forfeiture of the toss or the match, at the organizers' discretion.

🏆 5. Fair Play & Sportsmanship

  • 🤝Respect umpires, organizers, opponents and fellow players.
  • 🚫Avoid unnecessary arguments and disputes.
  • 😊Maintain a friendly and sporting atmosphere.
  • 📣Organizers may make necessary decisions to ensure smooth and timely completion.
  • 🚫Player Registration Rule: Once a player has played for one team, they cannot play for any other team in the same tournament.

🔥 Play hard, play fair, and enjoy the game! 🏏🏆
